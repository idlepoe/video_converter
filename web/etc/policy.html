Privacy Policy

WebP Converter Privacy Protection Policy

1. Purpose of Privacy Policy

WebP Converter (hereinafter "the App") establishes and discloses this privacy policy to protect users' personal information in accordance with relevant laws and regulations such as the Information and Communications Network Act and the Personal Information Protection Act, and to promptly and smoothly handle related complaints.

2. Purpose of Personal Information Processing

WebP Converter processes personal information for the following purposes:

  • Providing video conversion services
  • Improving app functionality and service quality
  • Handling customer inquiries and complaints

🔒 Important Privacy Protection Principles

WebP Converter does not collect any personal information directly.

• We do not collect personal identification information such as names, emails, or phone numbers.

• We do not collect trackable information such as location data or device information.

• We do not collect app usage statistics or analytics data.

• Note: Google AdMob may collect advertising-related data as described in Section 5.

3. Processing and Retention Period of Personal Information

WebP Converter does not collect personal information directly, so there is no processing or retention period for our app. However, Google AdMob may process advertising-related data according to their own privacy policy and retention periods.

4. Video File Processing Method

4.1 Local Processing Principle

WebP Converter processes users' video files as follows:

  • Local Processing: All video conversion tasks are performed only on the user's device.
  • No Server Transmission: Video files are not transmitted to external servers or cloud services.
  • Temporary Storage: Only uses the device's temporary storage during the conversion process and automatically deletes files after completion.

4.2 Gallery Storage

Converted videos are stored only in the user's device gallery and are not transmitted externally.

5. AdMob Advertising Service

5.1 Advertising Integration

WebP Converter uses Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. This service helps us provide the app free of charge to users.

5.2 AdMob Data Collection

Google AdMob may collect the following information for advertising purposes:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution
  • App Information: App version, installation source
  • Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID (can be reset by users)
  • Usage Data: Ad interactions, impressions, clicks

5.3 AdMob Privacy Policy

For detailed information about how Google AdMob handles data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy:

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.4 User Control

Users can control ad personalization through their device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising > Personalized Ads

6. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

WebP Converter does not collect personal information directly. However, Google AdMob may collect advertising-related data as described in Section 5.

7. Outsourcing of Personal Information Processing

WebP Converter does not outsource personal information processing tasks.

8. Rights, Obligations, and Exercise Methods of Information Subjects

WebP Converter does not collect personal information directly. For AdMob-related data, users can exercise their rights through Google's privacy controls as described in Section 5.4.

9. Security Measures for Personal Information

WebP Converter protects personal information through the following technical and administrative safeguards:

  • Minimizing data leakage risks through local processing
  • Processing only within the device without network transmission
  • Protecting data through automatic deletion of temporary files
  • Using Google's secure AdMob infrastructure for advertising data

The integration of AdMob advertising does not compromise our commitment to user privacy, as advertising data is handled by Google's secure infrastructure.
